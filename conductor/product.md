# Product Definition: Er Piotta

## Vision
Er Piotta is the central dashboard and intelligent assistant for the Carlesso family's school logistics at Tandem IMS (Zurich). It eliminates parental friction caused by scattered school communications, closed mobile apps, and complex daily school routines for Alessandro (Primary 3) and Sebastian (Primary 1).

## Core Capabilities
1. **Interactive School Dashboard (Astro Web App):**
   - Dedicated views for Alessandro (P3) and Sebastian (P1) displaying daily lesson timelines, teachers, and homework deadlines.
   - Dual family matrix view showing combined schedules side-by-side.
   - Color-coded parent duty tracking (Mamma / Papà dropoff and pickup rosters).
   - Dynamic morning/evening checklists (sports gear, reading logs, art aprons, Wochenplan).

2. **Piota App & Tandem Integration:**
   - Android emulator or direct API bridge to extract school announcements, notices, and calendar entries from the Piota mobile app.
   - Device registration and session handling for `palladiusbonton2@gmail.com`.

3. **Intelligent Automation (LLM Pipeline):**
   - Automated ingestion and summarization of school pub/sub messages and newsletters.
   - Direct synchronization with family calendars and creation of actionable to-do items.

## Target Users
- Parents (Riccardo & Kate), Childcare (Silvana), Children (Alessandro & Sebastian).
