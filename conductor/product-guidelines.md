# Product Guidelines: Er Piotta

## Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Encouraging, pragmatic, lighthearted (with Ermete Bottazzi spirit), and family-oriented.
- **Language:** Bilingual by design (Italian primary for home/family logistics, English/German terms preserved for school subjects and teachers: e.g. *Wochenplan*, *Kartaus*, *Primary 3*).
- **Clarity First:** Morning checklists must be unambiguous and actionable in 3 seconds or less.

## Design & UI Principles
- **Glanceable & High Contrast:** Immediate visual recognition of today's schedule, deadlines, and duties.
- **Color-Coded Logistics:**
  - 🟣 **Mamma (Kate):** Purple highlights (Mon, Tue, Fri).
  - 🟡 **Papà (Riccardo):** Yellow/Amber highlights (Wed, Thu).
  - 🧒 **Sebastian (P1):** Emerald/Cyan accents.
  - 👦 **Alessandro (P3):** Indigo/Sky blue accents.
- **Responsive & Lightweight:** Optimized for both mobile viewports and tablet/desktop wall dashboards.

## Reliability & Security
- **Privacy First:** School credentials, tokens, and child data must remain local/secure.
- **Fail-Safe Ingestion:** If the school app/emulator sync is unavailable, fallback gracefully to the static schedule.
